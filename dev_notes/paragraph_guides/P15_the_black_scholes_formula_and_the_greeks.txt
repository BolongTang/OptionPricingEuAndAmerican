SENTENCE PLAN (write in this order; the numbers in brackets are sentence counts)
  S1-S2  [2 sentences]  FOCUS: Formula: C = S0 Phi(d1) - K e^{-rT} Phi(d2) with d1, d2; it is the discounted Q-expectation of the lognormal call payoff; the put follows from parity.
  S3-S4  [2 sentences]  FOCUS: Greeks in closed form: Delta = Phi(d1), Gamma = phi(d1)/(S sigma sqrt T), vega = S phi(d1) sqrt T, rho = K T e^{-rT} Phi(d2), Theta; give the values at the money.
  S5-S6  [2 sentences]  FOCUS: Meaning of each Greek: delta the hedge ratio, gamma how fast the hedge changes and therefore how large the hedging error is, vega exposure to volatility, theta time decay, rho exposure to the rate.
  S7  [1 sentence]  FOCUS: Role as benchmark: the Black-Scholes price (macro BSprice) is the reference for every numerical result in Part B.

DRAFT SENTENCES CURRENTLY IN THE TEX FILE (placeholders to rewrite in your own words, one per line)
  S1. Evaluating $e^{-rT}\mathbb E^{\mathbb Q}[(S_T-K)^+]$ for the lognormal $S_T$ gives
  S2. \begin{equation}\label{eq:bs}  C=S_0\Phi(d_1)-Ke^{-rT}\Phi(d_2),\qquad
      d_{1,2}=\frac{\ln(S_0/K)+(r\pm\frac12\sigma^2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T}, \end{equation} and the put
      follows from put--call parity $C-P=S_0-Ke^{-rT}$.
  S3. The sensitivities are $\Delta=\Phi(d_1)$, $\Gamma=\varphi(d_1)/(S_0\sigma\sqrt T)$, $\mathcal
      V=S_0\varphi(d_1)\sqrt T$, $\rho=KTe^{-rT}\Phi(d_2)$ and
      $\Theta=-\frac{S_0\varphi(d_1)\sigma}{2\sqrt T}-rKe^{-rT}\Phi(d_2)$, where $\varphi$ is the
      standard normal density.
  S4. For the NVIDIA call they take the values $\Delta=\BSdelta$, $\Gamma=\BSgamma$, vega $\BSvega$
      (that is, \BSvegaPct\ dollars per volatility point), $\Theta=\BStheta$ per year and
      $\rho=\BSrho$.
  S5. Delta is the number of shares that hedges the option, gamma measures how quickly that number
      changes and therefore how large the hedging error is when rebalancing is discrete, and vega is
      the exposure to the volatility input, which is the least certain one.
  S6. Theta is the loss of value from the passage of time alone, about \BSthetaDay\ dollars per
      calendar day at the money, and rho the exposure to the interest rate.
  S7. The price \$\BSprice\ from Equation~\eqref{eq:bs} is the reference against which every
      numerical estimate in this part is measured.

DERIVATION / LATEX MATERIAL (use as the skeleton of the displayed equations)
Evaluate: $\mathbb E[(S_T-K)^+]=S_0e^{rT}\Phi(d_1)-K\Phi(d_2)$ with $S_T=S_0e^{(r-\sigma^2/2)T+\sigma\sqrt TZ}$ (complete the square in the Gaussian integral over $\{Z>-d_2\}$).
Delta: $\partial_SC=\Phi(d_1)$ (the terms from differentiating $d_1,d_2$ cancel: $S\varphi(d_1)=Ke^{-rT}\varphi(d_2)$).
Gamma: $\partial_S\Phi(d_1)=\varphi(d_1)/(S\sigma\sqrt T)$.  Vega: $\partial_\sigma C=S\varphi(d_1)\sqrt T$.
