DRAFT SENTENCES CURRENTLY IN THE TEX FILE (placeholders to rewrite in your own words, one per line)
  S1. At $t=1$ the walk equals $(2B-N)/\sqrt N$ with $B\sim\mathrm{Bin}(N,\tfrac12)$, so its
      distribution function is a step function that is known exactly.
  S2. We therefore compute the Kolmogorov distance $\sup_x|F_N(x)-\Phi(x)|$ exactly, checking both
      sides of every jump, with no sampling error.
  S3. Table~\ref{tbl:ks} shows that the distance decreases like $N^{-1/2}$: the product of the
      distance and $\sqrt N$ settles at \KSconst\ as $N$ grows.
  S4. This constant is $1/\sqrt{2\pi}=\varphi(0)$, half the probability mass of the central lattice
      cell, which is the size of the jump of the distribution function at its mode.
  S5. The histograms of Figure~\ref{fig:rwhist} show the lattice structure at $N_2=10$ and an almost
      perfect bell shape from $N_2=200$ on.
  S6. The log--log plot in Figure~\ref{fig:ksrate} has slope $-\tfrac12$, in agreement with the
      Berry--Esseen bound.
  S7. The error in the law of the terminal value is of order $N^{-1/2}$, which is slower than the
      $O(1/N)$ error of the CRR call price in Section~\ref{sec:B}, because the price averages a
      Lipschitz payoff over the lattice.

DERIVATION / LATEX MATERIAL (use as the skeleton of the displayed equations)
Exact CDF of the walk at t=1: $F_N(x_k)=\mathbb P(B\le k)$ at $x_k=(2k-N)/\sqrt N$.
KS distance: $\max_k\max\{|F_N(x_k)-\Phi(x_k)|,\ |F_N(x_k^-)-\Phi(x_k)|\}$.
Limit constant: the jump at the mode is $\mathbb P(B=N/2)\approx\sqrt{2/(\pi N)}$; half of it is $1/\sqrt{2\pi N}$.
