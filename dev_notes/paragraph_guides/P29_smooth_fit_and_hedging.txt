ROLE IN THE ARGUMENT

American price the cost of a replicating strategy up to the exercise date.

  S6-S7  [2 sentences]  FOCUS: Exact up to tau*; if the holder exercises suboptimally the seller keeps a surplus (the hedge super-replicates every exercise date); this is checked numerically in the tree.

DRAFT SENTENCES CURRENTLY IN THE TEX FILE (placeholders to rewrite in your own words, one per line)
  S1. The boundary $b(t)$ is part of the unknown, and the condition that fixes it is smooth fit:
      $\partial_xV$ is continuous across the boundary, so $\partial_xV(t,b(t))=-1$.
  S2. Heuristically, if $\partial_xV$ jumped at the boundary, the stopping rule could be improved by
      moving the boundary slightly.
  S3. A numerical solver never has to impose smooth fit, because the maximum in the tree recursion,
      or the projection in a finite-difference scheme, solves the obstacle problem directly.
  S4. Because the market is complete, the second fundamental theorem of asset pricing implies that
      the claim exercised at $\tau^*$ can be replicated by holding $\Delta_t=\partial_xV(t,S_t)$
      shares and the rest in the bond.
  S5. This is the delta hedge of the European option with the American value function: the formula
      is the same, the function is different, and $\Delta=-1$ in the stopping region because there
      $V=K-x$.
  S6. The replication is exact up to $\tau^*$, and if the holder exercises at any other time the
      seller is left with a surplus, so the hedge super-replicates every exercise date.
  S7. Section~\ref{sec:replication} checks this statement numerically in the tree.

DERIVATION / LATEX MATERIAL (use as the skeleton of the displayed equations)
Smooth fit heuristic: let $V_b(x)$ be the value of exercising at the first time $S\le b$. Then $\partial_bV_b(x)|_{b=b^*}=0$; for the perpetual put this is exactly $\frac d{db}[(K-b)b^\beta]=0$ (Section on the perpetual put).
Hedge: wealth $X_t=V(t,S_t)$ is self-financing while $S_t\in C$ because the $dt$-coefficient of $dV$ vanishes there.

PITFALLS / DO NOT SAY
'Replicable' is exact only up to the optimal exercise time. A holder who exercises late or early
leaves value with the seller.
