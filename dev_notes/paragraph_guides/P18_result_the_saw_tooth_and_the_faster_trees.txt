DRAFT SENTENCES CURRENTLY IN THE TEX FILE (placeholders to rewrite in your own words, one per line)
  S1. Figure~\ref{fig:saw} shows that the signed error alternates with the parity of $N$: $N$ times
      the error settles at $\SawEven$ for even $N$ and at $\SawOdd$ for odd $N$.
  S2. In absolute value both sequences decay like $1/N$, so parity changes the sign of the error but
      not its order.
  S3. Table~\ref{tbl:variants} compares seven trees at odd $N$.
  S4. At $N=101$ the Leisen--Reimer tree has an error of \LRerrHundred\ against \CRRerrHundredOdd\
      for plain CRR, and at $N=1001$ its error is \LRerrThousand.
  S5. On the log--log plot of Figure~\ref{fig:variants} plain CRR, Jarrow--Rudd and Tian follow
      slope $-1$ with visible oscillation, while averaging and smoothing remove most of the
      oscillation.
  S6. Only the Leisen--Reimer tree changes the slope, to $-2$.
  S7. For a European payoff with a kink the practical choice is therefore the Leisen--Reimer tree or
      smoothing combined with Richardson extrapolation.
  S8. For an American payoff we stay with plain CRR and control the error by refinement, because the
      accelerations rely on a closed-form value at the last step.
