DRAFT SENTENCES CURRENTLY IN THE TEX FILE (placeholders to rewrite in your own words, one per line)
  S1. The class \texttt{BrownianMotion} builds a path step by step from independent $N(0,\Delta t)$
      increments on $[0,1]$, with $N_1=10{,}000$ steps.
  S2. Five independent paths are drawn for the figure.
  S3. The subclass \texttt{symmetric\_random\_walk} overrides only the step, replacing the Gaussian
      draw by $\pm\sqrt{\Delta t}$ with probability one half each.
  S4. It is run with $N_2\in\{10,50,200,1000\}$ steps on the same time interval.
  S5. For the quantitative checks we add vectorised generators that draw 2000 paths per $N$ and
      compute quadratic and total variation.
  S6. The Kolmogorov distance and the law of the running maximum are computed exactly from the
      binomial distribution, so they carry no sampling error.
