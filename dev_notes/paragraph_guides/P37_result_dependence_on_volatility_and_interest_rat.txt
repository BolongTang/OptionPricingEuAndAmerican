  S5. For $r=0$ the boundary is empty in Table~\ref{tbl:amsr} because waiting never costs anything,

  S6. Exercising early would give up optionality and gain no interest.
  S7. The hedge ratio at the start, $\Delta(0,S_0)$, lies between \DeltaRangeLo\ and \DeltaRangeHi\
