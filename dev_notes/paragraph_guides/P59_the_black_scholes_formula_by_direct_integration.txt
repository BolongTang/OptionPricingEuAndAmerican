SENTENCE PLAN (write in this order; the numbers in brackets are sentence counts)
  S1-S2  [2 sentences]  FOCUS: Set-up: under Q S_T = S_0 exp((r - sigma^2/2)T + sigma sqrt(T) Z), Z standard normal; C = e^{-rT} E[(S_T - K)^+]; the payoff is positive exactly when Z > -d_2.
  S3  [1 sentence]  FOCUS: Split the expectation: C = e^{-rT}(E[S_T 1{Z > -d_2}] - K P(Z > -d_2)).
  S4  [1 sentence]  FOCUS: Second term: P(Z > -d_2) = Phi(d_2) by symmetry, giving K e^{-rT} Phi(d_2).
  S5-S6  [2 sentences]  FOCUS: First term: complete the square, e^{sigma sqrt(T) z} phi(z) = e^{sigma^2 T/2} phi(z - sigma sqrt(T)); the integral over z > -d_2 becomes Phi(d_2 + sigma sqrt(T)) = Phi(d_1).
  S7  [1 sentence]  FOCUS: Result and interpretation: C = S_0 Phi(d_1) - K e^{-rT} Phi(d_2); Phi(d_2) is the Q-probability of exercise and Phi(d_1) is the same probability after changing to the measure that takes the stock as numeraire.

DRAFT SENTENCES CURRENTLY IN THE TEX FILE (placeholders to rewrite in your own words, one per line)
  S1. Under $\mathbb Q$ write $S_T=S_0e^{(r-\frac12\sigma^2)T+\sigma\sqrt TZ}$ with $Z\sim N(0,1)$,
      so that $C=e^{-rT}\mathbb E[(S_T-K)^+]$.
  S2. The payoff is positive exactly when $Z>-d_2$, where
      $d_2=\frac{\ln(S_0/K)+(r-\frac12\sigma^2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T}$.
  S3. Splitting the expectation gives $C=e^{-rT}\big(\mathbb E[S_T\mathbf 1_{\{Z>-d_2\}}]-K\,\mathbb
      P(Z>-d_2)\big)$.
  S4. By symmetry $\mathbb P(Z>-d_2)=\Phi(d_2)$, so the second term is $Ke^{-rT}\Phi(d_2)$.
  S5. For the first term, completing the square gives $e^{\sigma\sqrt
      Tz}\varphi(z)=e^{\sigma^2T/2}\varphi(z-\sigma\sqrt T)$, so
  S6. \begin{equation}  \mathbb E[S_T\mathbf
      1_{\{Z>-d_2\}}]=S_0e^{rT}\int_{-d_2}^{\infty}\varphi(z-\sigma\sqrt
      T)\,dz=S_0e^{rT}\,\Phi(d_2+\sigma\sqrt T)=S_0e^{rT}\,\Phi(d_1). \end{equation}
  S7. Combining the two terms gives Equation~\eqref{eq:bs}: $\Phi(d_2)$ is the risk-neutral
      probability that the call ends in the money, and $\Phi(d_1)$ is the same probability under the
      measure that takes the stock as numeraire.
