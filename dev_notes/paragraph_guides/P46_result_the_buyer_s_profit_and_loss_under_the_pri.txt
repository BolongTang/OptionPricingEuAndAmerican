DRAFT SENTENCES CURRENTLY IN THE TEX FILE (placeholders to rewrite in your own words, one per line)
  S1. The buyer pays the fair value $V(0,S_0)=\AmVzero$ and exercises at the first date on which the
      path enters the exercise region.
  S2. Paths are simulated in the tree with the pricing weights $(p,1-p)$, because under the pricing
      measure the real-world drift $\mu$ plays no role.
  S3. The mean profit is \PnLmean\ with a standard error of \PnLse, consistent with zero, as it must
      be by the definition of the fair value.
  S4. A vectorised run with $10^5$ paths gives \PnLQmean\ with a standard error of \PnLQse, which
      confirms it.
  S5. The standard deviation of the profit is \PnLsd, the mean stopping time is \PnLtau\ years and
      the probability of exercising before maturity is \PnLpex.
  S6. Roughly half of the paths are therefore stopped early.
  S7. The distribution in Figure~\ref{fig:3a} has a point mass at $-V_0$ for the paths that end out
      of the money and a long right tail, and Table~\ref{tbl:pnlgrid} repeats the experiment on the
      nine $(\sigma,r)$ combinations.
  S8. The spread grows with $\sigma$ and shrinks with $r$, the probability of early exercise rises
      with $\sigma$, and for $r=0$ the option is never exercised early, so that $\tau=T$.
